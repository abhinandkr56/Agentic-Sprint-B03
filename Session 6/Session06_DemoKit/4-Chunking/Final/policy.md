# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Monthly plans
Monthly plans are not refunded once the month has started. If you were charged for a month you did not use, write to support within 48 hours and we will reverse the charge, once per account.

## Annual plans
Annual plans can be cancelled at any time. We refund the unused months, pro-rated, within 7 working days of your request.

## Free trial
The 14-day free trial needs no card. If you add a card and forget to cancel, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of the first charge.

## Downgrades
Moving from Pro to Starter takes effect on your next billing date. The price difference for the current period is not refunded, and users above the Starter limit are removed on that date.

## Upgrades
Moving from Starter to Pro takes effect immediately. You pay only the difference for the days left in the current period.

## Add-ons
Extra storage and SMS packs are prepaid and cannot be refunded, even if you cancel your subscription.

## Double charges
If you are charged twice for the same invoice, the extra payment is refunded automatically within 5 working days. You do not need to contact us.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds always go back to the original payment method. Card refunds can take up to 10 working days to appear on your statement; UPI refunds usually arrive the same day. We cannot refund to a different account.

## Customers outside India
If you paid in US dollars or euros, we refund in the same currency. The amount may differ slightly because of your bank's exchange rate.

## Chargebacks
If you dispute a payment with your bank instead of contacting us, your account is paused until the dispute is closed. Please talk to us first: it is almost always faster.

## Contact
Refund requests go to billing@example.com or the chat button in the app. We reply within one working day.
